Australia vs World: CO2 Emission Overview

Carbon dioxide (CO2) is a greenhouse gas that significantly contributes to global warming and climate change. It mainly originates from burning fossil fuels, including coal, oil, and natural gas, for energy production, transportation, and industry. This visualization aims to introduce CO2 emissions and provide a comparison of Australia’s emissions against the rest of the world. By exploring CO2 trends and emissions data, we can better understand Australia's role in the global emissions landscape and highlight opportunities for mitigation.
